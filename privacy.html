Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Slop Factory ("the App") is a personal desktop application used by its operator to upload original videos to the operator's own TikTok accounts.

1. Data we collect

The App collects no data from any third party. It has no users other than its operator, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

2. TikTok account data

When the operator connects a TikTok account through TikTok's official login (OAuth) flow, the App receives an access token and basic profile information (display name) for that account. This is used solely to upload videos to that account.

3. Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer. No data is transmitted to any server other than TikTok's official APIs. No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone.

4. Data deletion

Authorization can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Apps and websites), which invalidates the stored tokens. Local tokens can be deleted by removing the App's configuration file.
