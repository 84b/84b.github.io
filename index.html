Slop Factory

A personal desktop tool for producing and publishing original short-form quiz videos.

What it does

Slop Factory renders original quiz videos (voiceover, captions, music, and graphics) on the operator's computer and uploads the finished videos to the operator's own TikTok account using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Who it's for

It is a single-operator tool: it runs locally, connects only to accounts the operator owns via TikTok's official login, and is not offered as a service to anyone else.

Privacy in one line

No analytics, no tracking, no third-party data — access tokens stay on the operator's own machine.
